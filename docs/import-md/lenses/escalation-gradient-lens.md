\# Escalation Gradient Lens

\## Definition

Measures structural growth or intensification across sequential
executions.

\## Structural Transform

Input:

\- Sequential structural graphs

Operation:

\- Compare structural scope progression

\- Identify monotonic expansion

Output:

\- Escalation classification

\- Growth trajectory (if present)

\## Input Requirements

\- Comparable sequential records

\## Structural Signature of Failure

Failure exists when structural scope increases without declared
constraint.

\## Structural Limits

Evaluates structural growth only.

Does not evaluate authority legitimacy.
