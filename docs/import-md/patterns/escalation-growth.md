\# Escalation Growth

\## Definition

Structural changes intensify in magnitude or scope across sequential
evaluations.

\## Structural Signature

\- Sequential structural states

\- Increasing scope or impact

\- No declared limiting mechanism

\## Deterministic Test

Given sequential structural states, escalation must be governed by
declared constraints.

If escalation increases without declared limitation, Escalation Growth
exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate authority legitimacy.

Evaluates growth progression only.
