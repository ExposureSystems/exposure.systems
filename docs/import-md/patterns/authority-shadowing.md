\# Authority Shadowing

\## Definition

A declared authority is functionally overridden by another authority
without explicit declaration.

\## Structural Signature

\- Two authority layers

\- Effective control exercised by non-primary authority

\- No declared override rule

\## Deterministic Test

If effective governance differs from declared hierarchy without explicit
override, Authority Shadowing exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate hierarchy correctness.

Evaluates structural override only.
