\# Unconstrained Expansion

\## Definition

Structural regions expand without governing constraints limiting growth.

\## Structural Signature

\- Structural growth

\- Absence of limiting constraints

\- No defined termination boundary

\## Deterministic Test

Given declared constraints, structural growth must be bounded.

If growth continues without constraint, Unconstrained Expansion exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate correctness of constraints.

Evaluates existence of growth limits only.
