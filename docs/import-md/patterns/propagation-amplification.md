\# Propagation Amplification

\## Definition

Structural declarations increase in scope or intensity as they
propagate.

\## Structural Signature

\- Propagation chain

\- Increasing structural effect

\- No declared amplification rule

\## Deterministic Test

Given propagation rules, structural impact must remain within declared
bounds.

If impact amplifies beyond declared limits, Propagation Amplification
exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate propagation intent.

Evaluates amplification behavior only.
