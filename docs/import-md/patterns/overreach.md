\# Overreach

\## Definition

Structural actions extend beyond declared scope or authority.

\## Structural Signature

\- Declared scope

\- Structural expansion outside scope

\- No declared authorization

\## Deterministic Test

Given declared scope, structural modifications must remain within
bounds.

If exceeded, Overreach exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate authority correctness.

Evaluates scope adherence only.
