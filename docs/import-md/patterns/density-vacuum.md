\# Density Vacuum

\## Definition

Structural concentration decreases sharply within a region expected to
maintain density.

\## Structural Signature

\- Sparse or empty structural region

\- Missing expected nodes or edges

\- Declared baseline density unmet

\## Deterministic Test

Given declared density expectations, structural regions must meet
minimum density.

If a region falls below declared thresholds, Density Vacuum exists.

\## Structural Limits

Does not evaluate structural correctness.

Evaluates density absence only.
