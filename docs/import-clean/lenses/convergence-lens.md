# Convergence Lens

## Definition

Compares parallel structural systems to determine whether they align
under shared authority.

## Structural Transform

Input:

- Two or more structural graphs

- Shared authority context

Operation:

- Compare structural graphs across systems

- Identify divergence

Output:

- Convergence classification

- Divergent regions (if present)

## Input Requirements

- Comparable structural scope

- Shared authority definitions

## Structural Signature of Failure

Failure exists when structurally comparable systems produce
non-equivalent graphs.

## Structural Limits

Evaluates cross-system equivalence only.

Does not evaluate internal determinism.
